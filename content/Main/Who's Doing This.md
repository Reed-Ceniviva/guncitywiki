## And How I Got Here (9/25/26)


My name is Reed. PAL helped me and my partner deal with the grief of slowly losing our sick cat, they brought some laughter into our home while we were mourning. We had listened to PAL a bit before that, my partner introduced me to them and we would listen to episodes on the drive to work, but it was on constantly during this period. After the grieving I kept listening to PAL going back into older episodes, checking out their skits on youtube, and eventually finding ep 100 Gun City Operation Solutio Dark Brain. My partner had tried to get me into the D&D series (Trebuchet Village) before but after finding the beginning of it all I felt better prepared to jump into the rest of them.

For about 6 months now I've been listening through all the D&D series on the Patreon, Going back and relistening, finding Sullivan's Quest, The Sullivan Quest, and Sawcast About Tricks, and even going through the Chapo Trap House D&D one offs Patches has DM'ed. Through all of it I've loved the world that Patrick has created and the framework he has provided and incrementally improved for the entertainment of his players and the listeners.  

I think in sophtlosophers crystal Patrick talks about wishing the Wiki for the D&D campaigns was still up and it has been sticking in my mind every time I listen to an episode, wishing I could read about how a location or building works into the overall world that Patches has designed. So instead of just listening to the episodes on repeat like someone who enjoys The Office, I've decided to start building a Wiki.

about a month ago I became acquainted with the note taking software Obsidian. The main draw this app gets is that it allows you to link files together easily and it has a built in graphing function that shows these files and links as a cluster of nodes connected by lines that can be very intriguing. There should be a small version of this as part of the sidebar if you're viewing this on a webpage. This page linking functionality allows Obsidian to ask as the framework to a personal wiki, some people use this to create what's called a "second brain" or an archive of the knowledge a person has learned/taken notes on. This is something I think would be really interesting to do if I were still in school, but I'm not the note taking type so I didn't see myself using it much for that purpose. 

While looking around the settings of Obsidian I did come across a setting relating to exporting files so that they are optimized to be used online for a wiki which reminded me about PAL's D&D's lack of a wiki and I decided to explore the possibility of making one. With a few google searches I was able find examples of Wikipedias hosted for free through Github Pages, and now knowing it was free I was comfortable starting on the idea by working on part 1 and 2 of PAL Ep 100 Gun City Operation Solutio Darkbrain. 

Initially I just played the audio at 25% speed and wrote out almost everything that was said. I had it slowed down so much so I could add the name of who was talking and add links to later turn into pages, then would go back and fill out the information in those pages and add formatting to the episode to break it into sections for more clarity. I continued this process into Uncle's Falcon where I felt like I had gotten a rhythm for the process, but despite this it was still about an 8 hour endeavor per episode. 

While I am a stay at home pet parent this was still not manageable for me to keep up with my daily tasks but still wanting to be able to complete an episode a day. Knowing the transcribing was the largest chunk of time required for the current process, and also knowing Jubio or Cameron has used [[Movavi Auto Subtitling Tool]] (Or was it Davinci's?) for movie Monday's, I started downloading Movavi to try and cut out that bottleneck. So now I can get the subtitles file from Movavi that I can clean with regex commands and then I finally have a transcription, a pretty shitty transcription riddled with inconsistent spelling errors and no formatting, but still a lot less typing. 

Having completed Solutio Dark Brain and Uncle's Falcon, I decided to start figuring putting this online. I got Quartz 5 cloned and walked through the process of connecting the Gun City Wiki documents from Obsidian to the GitHub using it. I think it's working now. The syncing doesn't seem to work how i think and I have to completely rebuild the site every time I want to update it, but I can work that into the system of things.

I'll be happy to hand this over to PAL if they ever want to host it themselves but for now I'm happy to plug away adding to this and building it out myself. I guess since its on github someone can clone it and make a pull request with changes or files or whatever to add to this, but I'm no github wizard so I'm mostly gonna be trying not to break this other than updating it.
