[[Leo the Collector]]'s Home
A massive sky island over Gun City

~2x2 mile size


Locations:
- Hospital
	- state of the art
	- run by Jollibee Nurse Joys
- Private School
- Gym
	- offers both CrossFit and CyberCrossFit
- Mansion
	- has many of Leo's artifacts on display
- Vehicle Hanger/helipad
	- Where [[Leo's Sky Ship]] is parked
- Shopping Centre
	- Has a Brookstone
- Event Center
	- The event center is capable of reconfiguring itself into all sorts of event spaces that allows Leo to host any kind of event he wants to throw.


Is the location of an auction held by Leo that included a floppy disc with a clue to the location of the Penguin of doom or the Temple in which it's held, specifically in the Event Center.
