PSP = Phone Super Phone


- Communication Device
- Can connect calls between people in and out of [[Da Matrix 2]]
- Can display maps
- can receive calls
- can receive texts
- can be plugged into VR setups
- can access discord

for most purposes it's just a smart phone, but can fill the place of most handheld future technology for the sake of progressing the plot
