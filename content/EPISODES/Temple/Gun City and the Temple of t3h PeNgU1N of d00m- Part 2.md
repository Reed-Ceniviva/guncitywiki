<h3 align="center">Recap</h3>

Last time on [[Gun City]], the Temple of Teh penguin of Doom. Three brilliant archaeologists, [[Patrick Yoda]], [[Blotoss Piss]], and [[Ray William Johnson]], were hired out of prison by a one [[Leo the Collector]], one of the richest people in Gun City, who is an avid collector of rare artifacts from [[Ebaum|Planet Ebaum]]'s ancient past. He brought our heroes via his sky galleon to his island in the sky, [[Sky England]], to ask them to help him find a long fabled [[N-Word Pass]] of the lost [[Tamagotchi Empire]], located in the undiscovered [[The Temple of Teh Penguin of Doom|Temple of Teh Penguin of Doom]]. To do so, they will need to need a key into discovering its location, which Leo has arranged the obtainment of by holding an auction on his island in Where one of the items up for grabs, the floppy disk, "The Penguin of Doom", he said to contain clues as to where to find the Temple. [[Sullivan The Frog]], assistant to Leo will be representing the collector at the auction, and the gang will have to protect him from many dangerous folks who also desire the item. Whether they are [[Gerard De Poupu]], rival collector to Leo, or [[Pennsylvania Jim]], rival adventurer to the gang. After fucking a couch for a second time, spending a day stealing [[Techno Slime]] percocets from a hospital in Sky England, and stealing the time of a sales rep at a Brookstone at Leo's shopping mall, our heroes made their way to the event center, where they met the aforementioned troublesome duo of de Poupu, and Pennsylvania Jim, along with Blotoss and Ray's ex-lover [[Marie Stain]], and her bodyguard [[Rex]]. After getting right plastered and all that, the fellows settled down for the auction, but not before taking note of a strange figure in a hood sitting quietly among the crowd. About halfway through the auction, Patrick Yoda with his super hearing heard what he thought was Jafar sneaking around the center. He and Blotoss Piss went out to investigate while Ray William Johnson held down the fort of the auction house. Blotoss sent [[Monkey named Patrick]] up the air vents where he confirmed the worst, there were party crashes about. Blotoss helped the strategically tiny Patrick Yoda up through the vent to learn more, and Patrick Yoda assessed where in the ventilation system the intruders had gone. Earlier, Blotoss had managed to sneak a set of Volkswagen Jetta keys into the auction room, buying Patrick Yoda just enough time to use Yoda's mind, reading the thoughts of the intruders with such incredible clarity that he breached the language barrier, to understand with certainty the intent of these foreign invaders, to steal the floppy disk. Thinking fast, Patrick Yoda busted down the grate of the vent, whipped out his hoverboard and brutalized these intruders, beginning a brawl in the auction house.
