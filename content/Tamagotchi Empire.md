Leo talking about the Tamagotchi Civilization:
before Earthlings arrived on Planet [[Ebaum]], there were many a civilization, but none as storied or as lost to time as the [[Tamagotchi (Race)]] people. Thought of as almost mystical in nature, recent discoveries have been made that suggest they do indeed exist. That said, there isn't a ton of scholarship on it. But you know that the Tamagotchi people were famous for being really needy and much of their infrastructure helped sort of lay the groundwork for many of the smaller city-states outside of Gun City and on the rest of Planet Ebaum.
