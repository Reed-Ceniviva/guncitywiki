Also known as Jeff The Third Reich

Suckla talking about Jeff: *he's one of the biggest players in Da Matrix 2. He funds a lot of the [[V-tech]] that lives there.  Jeff is my reply guy, nothing more. But he seems to think he's a bit more than that. My runners I send through there, he starting to stop them in their tracks. He's starting to prop them up as captures he's made in glorious ways of fighting corruption.*

One of the biggest players in  [[Da Matrix 2]], he runs/controls a lot of the [[V-tech]] inside the simulation. In Da Matrix 2 he lives at the top of [[Mount Pole]] in a giant pagoda where he and his posse hang out in kimonos.


Jeff's Pagoda in Da Matrix 2 was ransacked by his own followers after [[Blotoss Piss]] Disguised himself as Jeff and told the followers they could take everything and blow it up. This was part of a mission Blowtoss and the other detectives from the [[Top Tens Tattletale Agency]] were on as part of payment to the [[Mad Suckla]] to get into Da Matrix 2.

Jeff was aware of the plans for [[The Cockulus Rift 2.0- The Cockulus Quest Cordless Experience]]
