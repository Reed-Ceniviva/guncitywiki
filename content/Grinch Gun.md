A big Chris Kyle sniper rifle style Santa Clause Special snub nose revolver with a scoundrel scope attachment for long distance grinch hunting. It's possible to reload the gun by spinning it around like a Winchester.

The gun is described as a snub nose in Darkbrain but more like a sniper rifle in Uncle's Falcon

Ammo: "shoots little twangy, Seussian looking bullets"
